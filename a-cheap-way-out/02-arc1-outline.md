# A Cheap Way Out — Arc 1 Chapter Outline

28 chapters at about 3,300–3,800 words each, roughly 98,000 words in total.

Each chapter below gives its **Objective** (what Oren wants this chapter), its **Turn**, and its **Hook**. ⚡ marks a use of the power, ✖ a snap-back, and ♠ a Forced Bet event.

---

## Part One: Cold Storage (Ch 1–8)

**Ch 1: Delivery Fee**
- **Objective:** Deliver a late order to a condemned walk-up behind the cold-stores without losing his last rating star. The customer is refusing to pay and is blocking the stairwell.
- **Turn:** Tully Brask leans on him, and his palms burn a hand-print into Oren's delivery bag. Oren hesitates over whether to catch the swinging stair door, and an involuntary ⚡ ripple fires: let the door go → it slams → Tully flinches → he can't feel his grip on the rail (Brand's cost) → spilled soup on the landing. Oren lets the door go. Tully falls three flights onto exposed rebar and dies.
- **Hook:** Two phones buzz at once. Oren's Hopline app reads: *Customer reported order not received. Your GPS has been logged.* Tully's phone shows a message from "UNCLE H": *did the driver kid say yes?*

**Ch 2: Cold Storage**
- **Objective:** Make the body and the evidence disappear before dawn.
- **Turn:** He drags Tully into an abandoned mine cold-store. A second involuntary ripple fires over a light switch. He refuses to act on it and gets his first on-page ✖ snap-back: a nosebleed, a white-out, and a rusted shelving rack comes down and pins his arm. He gets free with a gashed forearm. A girl on a bike watches from the canal path (Wen, though he doesn't know her yet). He keeps Tully's phone, because it's the only way to know what the people looking for Tully know.
- **Hook:** In Tully's jacket he finds a folded job sheet. The "DRIVER" slot has "Cheap (Kalt?) — ask him" pencilled in. Tully wasn't robbing him. Tully was trying to hire him.

**Ch 3: Placement Value: Nil**
- **Objective:** Get through his supervision check-in and sort out the Hopline complaint without anyone looking closely at his arm or his night.
- **Turn:** This chapter carries the world. Ms. Ollery's terminal shows the garnishee arithmetic, his Bureau certificate (*PC-H, Instability 4, placement value: nil*) and the grey stamp. He lies to Hopline's chat-bot support and wins back half a star. In the stairwell of his building, a neighbour bets him 20 marl that the lift is broken again, in front of two aunties. That's the ♠ low-stakes demonstration: he accepts without thinking and loses 20 marl, and the reader learns the rule from how fast he says yes.
- **Hook:** Nan says, without looking up from her pickles: "A big polite boy came by. Bram. He said Tully told him you'd be expecting him."

**Ch 4: The Yard**
- **Objective:** Walk into the crew's scrapyard and come out alive, with nobody suspecting him.
- **Turn:** We meet Hesk, Ludo, Saint, Cobb and Bram through what they're doing (sorting stolen pharmacy stock, arguing about the tea). Oren lies: he says he left the food at the door and Tully never answered. Hesk is worried but sentimental. Tully goes on benders, so Hesk will give it three days. Since Tully vouched for him, Hesk offers Oren the driver slot. Ludo needles him. Oren takes the job because refusing would look like guilt.
- **Hook:** Wen, the lookout, walks him out to the gate and says quietly: "That was your bike on the canal path. Two in the morning. I listen to things."

**Ch 5: Echo**
- **Objective:** Neutralise the only witness.
- **Turn:** He follows Wen and sees her real situation: her mother is an addict, and Wen runs the household like a small business. He finds out what Wen actually heard: the slam, the fall, and nothing that proves anything. He tries to trigger the power deliberately for the first time ⚡ (his hand on a teacup, asking himself what happens if he tips it). It works, and he learns he can choose his triggers. He offers Wen a deal: a quarter of his cut, and he'll teach her to drive properly. Her agenda is to be treated as an adult earner, and she accepts.
- **Hook:** He gets home and Sergeant Mott is sitting in Nan's kitchen, eating a boiled sweet from her jar.

**Ch 6: Sergeant Mott**
- **Objective:** Lie to the police.
- **Turn:** Mott is dry, patient and terrifyingly ordinary. He hasn't come about Tully officially. He's come about the gashed arm the Reform nurse logged at the free clinic. Oren turns his flaw into a trick. He leans hard on his Bureau classification ("unstable hunches, adverse episodes, I walk into shelving, it's in my file") and makes his own unreliability the alibi. Mott half buys it. Mott also lets slip that he's on Hesk's payroll and that Hesk has asked him to find Tully, quietly.
- **Hook:** Mott says: "Tully's phone was on for three hours after he went missing. Moving around. Funny thing for a dead man's phone to do. Or a man on a bender." Then he leaves his card.

**Ch 7: Wheelman**
- **Objective:** Drive the first job, robbing a licensed dispensary's rime transfer van, and come home paid.
- **Turn:** This is the first action set piece and shows the crew's powers in use (Cobb's Tumbler, Ludo's Ballast, Wen's Echo). The chase goes wrong. Oren reads two ripples back to back ⚡⚡ and can only act on one. ✖ The snap-back blinds him at the wheel for eight seconds, and a cyclist he could have warned is clipped and hurt. They get away. His cut is 3,200 marl. He pays 400 of it to Nan for "rent" and 800 to the supervision office, which is the first payment that makes Ms. Ollery look up.
- **Hook:** In the van's paperwork, Saint finds a dispatch note about a "special-handling courier" coming through the Kettle Road truck stop on Thursday. Hesk says: "Thursday, then."

**Ch 8: Dead Man's Withdrawals**
- **Objective:** Move Tully's body. Imre Dask's *Coldwell Renewal* survey crews have started clearing the cold-stores for the Meridane data centre.
- **Turn:** This is the dark comedy set piece. Oren tells Bram he has a freezer of stolen fish to move. Bram helps carry a chest freezer full of "fish" (actually Tully) across town at night, with Bram happily planning his noodle stall the whole way. They put it in the Yard's broken back-room freezer, which nobody uses. The safest place for the body is in the house of the people looking for it. ⚡ One ripple gets a Watch patrol past them.
- **Hook:** Saint, doing the books, says flatly: "Someone's been withdrawing from Tully's account. In his signature. Badly." Oren hasn't touched it. So who has?

## Part Two: Lot Zero (Ch 9–14)

**Ch 9: Thursday**
- **Objective:** Plan the courier job so that Oren stays in the van, the safe seat.
- **Turn:** ♠ **Trouble trigger #1.** Ludo has noticed Oren's oddities and smells weakness. At the final briefing, in front of the whole crew, with the job forty minutes away, he says: "Two thousand says the new boy hasn't got the spine to do the grab himself." If Oren refuses, he's blind for the job. He accepts, and now he's the one who has to break into the courier's car.
- **Hook:** At the truck stop, a grey sedan parks. A tidy man in a pale coat gets out and walks into the diner, and he carefully wipes his fingers on a napkin before he touches the door.

**Ch 10: Lot Zero**
- **Objective:** Get into the car, grab the rime and get out.
- **Turn:** The boot holds a declared rime shipment (two kilos, a huge haul) and, underneath it, a small cold case. When Oren reaches for the case, his ⚡ reading goes **blank** for the first time. He feels white silence, like going deaf. Out of curiosity, and for no reason he could defend, he slips the case into his Hopline hot box instead of handing it to the crew. Glade comes out early. In their first exchange, Glade appraises him through the window ("Delivery? What band would they put you in?") and touches the hot box as Oren rides past (a pin). Oren gets away through a ⚡ ripple involving a waitress and a tray of drinks.
- **Hook:** At home, he opens the case: one vial of faintly blue liquid. The label reads *LANTERN — LOT 0 — NOT FOR AWAKENING TRIALS*, and it carries an Assay Bureau custody seal beside a Meridane serial. He can't sell it and he can't hand it in. He puts it in Nan's freezer, inside a bag of frozen dumplings.

**Ch 11: The Drive**
- **Objective:** Act normal: ride a full Hopline shift and find out what Lantern is.
- **Turn:** He delivers lunch to the Placement Drive at the Esterhal Institute. This is the world through prices: recruiters' tables, bond terms, Rung salaries, a former Reform-mate's kid sister signing a five-year bond at Meridane. He chats up the Meridane recruiter, Neve Ashcombe (Shelf-life), and learns that "Lantern" is a word that makes her go still. Meanwhile, the crew's two kilos make Hesk the happiest he's been in years, and make Ludo greedy.
- **Hook:** Hopline pushes a notice to all Coldwell riders: *Safety reminder: Rider found deceased near the canal. Please ride with care.* The dead rider is the one who serves Kettle Road. Glade followed the pin on the hot box, which Oren dumped in the canal, and then found the wrong rider.

**Ch 12: Depot**
- **Objective:** Find out what the man in the pale coat knows, without being recognised.
- **Turn:** Glade is at the Hopline depot, charming the dispatcher, Aunt Rosh. This is the first full scene with Glade: the plum stones, the expense log, the appraisals. He shakes everyone's hand. Oren dodges the handshake through a ⚡ ripple: he drops his phone and the dispatcher's dog goes for it. Glade's interest is polite, total and wrong, for now. Oren notices that Rosh keeps turning toward Glade without knowing she's doing it (the cost of Keep becoming visible).
- **Hook:** Glade asks Rosh for the Coldwell rider roster. Oren's name is on it, with the note *rating suspended pending complaint — Kettle Road area.*

**Ch 13: The Roster**
- **Objective:** Get his name off Glade's list.
- **Turn:** Saint forges a corrected roster in Rosh's hand and gets it swapped into the depot files. But Rosh is found dead in the morning (Glade's log: *Dispatcher, 1 hr*). Mott now has a missing nephew, a dead rider and a dead dispatcher. To take the heat off himself, Oren sets Ludo up. He feeds Mott the withdrawals from Tully's account: forged signature, Ludo's branch, Ludo's timing.
- **Hook:** Mott takes it to Hesk privately. Hesk calls the crew in that night, puts his pistol on the table and says, "Ludo, son. Sit."

**Ch 14: The Yard, Again** *(midpoint)*
- **Objective:** Survive the Ludo confrontation without the truth coming out.
- **Turn:** The confrontation never finishes, because Glade walks into the Yard. He traced the two kilos through the fence Ludo had already called. The gang and the psychopath collide here. Glade breaks Hesk's knee, kills Cobb in an ugly and quick way, and fights Bram, who loses and is badly hurt. He takes back the two kilos. Then he asks for "the other item". Nobody knows what he means except Oren. Glade gives them until tomorrow night and makes a point of touching Wen's cheek (a pin).
- **Hook:** As the gates close, Hesk turns to his ruined crew and says: "What other item?" Oren says nothing. In the back room, the broken freezer has started to hum unevenly.

## Part Three: The Blank (Ch 15–21)

**Ch 15: Inventory**
- **Objective:** Keep the case, keep his secret and keep the crew from scattering or giving him up.
- **Turn:** The aftermath: the cost to bodies, Cobb's funeral money, and Bram unable to lift a kettle. Saint works out from Glade's word "item" that someone in the crew is holding back, and she looks at Oren. Oren decides to keep Lantern. It's the only leverage he will ever hold, and near it his head is quiet for the first time in three years. Meanwhile, Ludo is running scared and is now loose in town with a grudge.
- **Hook:** Wen, listening with Echo for Glade's voice, hears him on a phone: "No, it isn't with the crew. It's with someone who doesn't know what it's worth. That's always the cheapest person in the room."

**Ch 16: Compass**
- **Objective:** Understand Glade's ability before he understands Oren's.
- **Turn:** Wen keeps turning to face the same direction. Bram does too. Oren works out that Keep pulls both ways, so the pinned people are compasses. He tests it ⚡, and the ripples confirm it, but they go blank whenever he carries the case. He realises the vial blanks Glade too, which is why Glade hunts by asking questions and killing.
- **Hook:** Nan is standing at her pickle stall, absent-mindedly turned toward the canal. She says: "Nice man this morning. Bought lime pickle. Held my hand to count the change."

**Ch 17: Nan Won't Move**
- **Objective:** Get Nan out of Glade's reach.
- **Turn:** This is the comedy chapter. Nan won't leave her stall before the lottery draw, and Oren has to fake a minor prize to get her moving. He hides her in the flat right next to the freezer with the vial in it, and her pin goes dark. Then, mid-chapter, he realises what that means: Glade now knows the item is *wherever Nan disappeared*. Oren has given him the address.
- **Hook:** At 2 a.m., someone knocks on the flat door with three polite, evenly spaced knocks.

**Ch 18: Rime**
- **Objective:** Get out of the flat alive with Nan and the case.
- **Turn:** He can't read through the blank. He has to leave the case to see, and leaving the case unblanks Nan. For the first time he takes street rime from Wen's mother's stash. The horizon stretches to an hour and the stones swim. ⚡⚡⚡ He gets them out down the laundry chute and across the roofs. The escape works, but it costs him: ✖ one ripple he can't begin snaps back, and Nan's ankle breaks on the fire stairs. The drug treated three ways in one chapter: Wen's mother (a logistics problem), Hesk's tremors (systemic), and Ludo's cut product found on the street (cruel).
- **Hook:** Oren hides Nan with Saint. Then he calls the number from the fence and makes Glade an offer: the item, tomorrow, at the depot.

**Ch 19: The Swap**
- **Objective:** Sell Glade a fake.
- **Turn:** Saint forges a perfect case and label. In her apartment Oren sees the drift of her signature on her own rent book, which tells him her loyalty has a price too. At the depot, Glade picks up the fake and feels it with Keep. The real one is blank and this one isn't, so he knows instantly. The swap turns into a fight. Bram, half-healed, takes a beating. Oren gets out with Bram, but Glade gets Wen. He was always following her pin.
- **Hook:** Glade's message: *A real one for a real one. You have until the shift change. — S.G. (P.S. What band were you? I'd guess nil.)*

**Ch 20: House Rules**
- **Objective:** Take command of what's left of the crew, because Wen's rescue needs everyone.
- **Turn:** ♠ **The exploit.** Hesk, on crutches and drugged, still holds the crew's loyalty and wants to give Glade everything he can. Oren needs Hesk's people and Hesk's guns, so he gets Hesk to offer *him* a bet in front of Saint and Bram: "Bet my yard you can't get her back by sunrise." Oren has engineered the bet so that Hesk's pride makes him put up the Yard as the stake, and Oren accepts instantly. Now the crew is his for the night, by Hesk's own word. He also uses Hesk's systemic addiction against him, and he isn't proud of it.
- **Hook:** Saint pulls him aside. "Ludo's been to the Watch. And to Glade. He's sold them both your name."

**Ch 21: Ludo's Price**
- **Objective:** Turn Ludo's betrayal into the delivery mechanism.
- **Turn:** Ludo has told Glade two things: that the delivery boy has the item, and that the boy *can't say no to a bet*. Ludo pieced it together from Chapter 9 and from Hesk's own bet in Chapter 20. Oren catches Ludo. Ludo uses Ballast in the fight, and its cost sends him flying when Bram shoulders him. Oren leaves Ludo alive with a message to carry back to Glade. Glade kills the messenger anyway, and Ludo's death is on Oren too.
- **Hook:** When Oren gets back to the Yard, the back room smells. Hesk is standing in front of the open freezer.

## Part Four: One Hour (Ch 22–28)

**Ch 22: What's in the Freezer**
- **Objective:** Survive Hesk.
- **Turn:** This is the emotional peak, delivered quietly. Hesk, with a gun, learns that Oren killed Tully. Oren tells the truth: the door, the soup, the rail. Hesk does not shoot him. Instead: "You'll bring that girl back. Then you'll dig. Then we'll see." Hesk also says something that matters for Arc 2: Tully once told him the Bureau re-tests "adverse" kids from the Reform "quietly, for a list".
- **Hook:** Glade's terms arrive. The handover is at the Coldwell Pit, the old mine head, at shift change, and Oren must come alone.

**Ch 23: Small Things, Placed**
- **Objective:** Prepare the battlefield before he gets there.
- **Turn:** Oren plans for the dark hour. He spends the day walking Coldwell and committing ⚡ to small actions: he loosens a grate, swaps a bus timetable card, unlatches a dog's gate, props a fire door, changes the time on Mott's appointment card, and leaves Hesk's pistol on Bram's tray. Each is a ripple he has read and begun, so the window is satisfied. They'll run on without him. He also uses Mott: he tips Mott anonymously that the dispatcher's killer will be at the Pit at shift change. A mistake: he misjudges one stone (Saint).
- **Hook:** Saint isn't where she promised to be. Her flat is empty except for her old school notebook, opened to a page where she has practised Oren's signature.

**Ch 24: The Pit**
- **Objective:** Make the exchange and get Wen.
- **Turn:** Glade, Wen and a terrible courtesy. ♠ **Trouble trigger #2.** Glade, in front of Wen and two Meridane hired hands, says: "Here's a wager. Your item against her. I bet you can't walk to me without your little hunches." Accepting means walking into his reach. Refusing switches off his power. Oren refuses, and the dark hour begins.
- **Hook:** The ripples stop. Glade smiles like a man whose quote has been accepted. Then, somewhere above the pit, a dog Oren set loose six hours earlier starts barking.

**Ch 25: One Hour**
- **Objective:** Stay alive for sixty minutes without the power.
- **Turn:** The pre-set chains land one after another, without his foresight: the dog, the bus, the grate, the fire door, Mott arriving forty minutes early because of the altered card. Oren realises in the middle of the fight that he never saw the future. He made it. That is the partial reveal. Saint's betrayal turns out to be a stone in the stream. She sold Oren's forged signature on a confession to Meridane's lawyers, and she isn't at the Pit.
- **Hook:** Glade, bleeding, finds the one part of the plan Oren couldn't place, which is Oren himself. He has Oren's throat in his hand with eleven minutes of the dark hour left.

**Ch 26: Bram**
- **Objective:** Win.
- **Turn:** Bram walks in with the pistol from the tray, a chain Oren set and didn't fully read. Bram takes the shot meant for Oren and doesn't get up. Glade dies in the brine sump below the mine head, pulled under by an ore-bucket counterweight that a loosened bolt released. Oren pulled that bolt that morning without being able to see what it would do. Wen is alive. The dark hour ends, and the first ripple Oren reads afterwards is pointless and small: whether to close Bram's eyes.
- **Hook:** Mott's torch comes over the lip of the pit. "Kalt. Hands where I can see them. Then tell me a story I'll like."

**Ch 27: Paperwork**
- **Objective:** Make the bodies make sense to the state.
- **Turn:** Oren gives Mott a story that suits Mott's pension. Glade killed Rosh and the rider. Ludo killed Tully, and the body was found in the Yard freezer, with Saint's forged withdrawals to support it. Ludo, conveniently, is dead. Hesk takes his cut and his medical retirement and quietly hands Oren the Yard: the bet, honoured. Mott accepts the story and names his price, a monthly cut, for good. Wen and Bram's mother are at the funeral. A polite Meridane caller on Tully's still-active phone asks whether "our contractor" has checked in. Oren hangs up.
- **Hook:** A letter from the Assay Bureau: *Reassessment requested. PC-H classification under review. Please attend with any materials in your possession.*

**Ch 28: A Cheap Way Out**
- **Objective:** Count what he has and what it cost.
- **Turn:** The final accounting, in Oren's voice. He has the Yard, a crew (Wen, a limping Hesk who advises, Bram's empty noodle-stall plan), the supervision debt paid in full, Mott on the books, the vial and a name Meridane now knows. He opens the freezer, holds Lantern and tries to read a ripple that touches it. For one second the blank isn't white.
- **Hook (series):** In the half-second it opens, he sees his own name on a list that isn't the Bureau's, and next to it the Roll's watermark. Then the blank comes back. End of Arc 1.

## What the Win Costs

- **Won:** Glade dead, Wen alive, the Yard his, the debt cleared, Lantern kept.
- **Lost:** Bram dead. Nan injured, and she has seen what he is. He betrayed Hesk's trust (twice) and he's in Mott's pocket for good. Meridane and the Bureau both know his name, and his reassessment is coming.
- **Still open:** what Lantern is, why it blanks, what his power really is, the Bureau's quiet list, and the Roll.
